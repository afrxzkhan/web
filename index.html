🔒 Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026
Last Updated: November 15, 2026
Application Name: Burhan Rem: Quran Hifz (iOS & Android)

At Burhan Rem (developed by Afroz Khan), we believe that memorizing and reading the Holy Quran should be a completely private, distraction-free spiritual experience. This Privacy Policy details how information is handled when you install and use the Burhan Rem application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (No Account Required)

Burhan Rem does not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide your real name, phone number, location, or any other Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to use the app. We do not maintain external user databases, and we never sell, rent, or monetize user information.

2. Local On-Device Storage Only

All app activity and user preferences are stored strictly locally on your physical device using standard local storage (localStorage / Capacitor Preferences). This locally stored data includes:

  • Your earned stars, blue diamond mastery badges, and daily login streaks.
  • Your Spaced Repetition System (SRS) schedules, interval steps (Days 1, 3, 7, 14, 30), and review dates for each Mushaf page.
  • Your preferred language mode (Arabic, English, Arabic + English, or Urdu), reciter selection, and optional display profile name.

Because this data never leaves your phone or tablet, clearing your app data or uninstalling the application will permanently delete your locally stored progress.

3. Audio Streaming & Network Usage

Burhan Rem bundles the 15-line Indo-Pak Quran text, fonts, and layout coordinates locally inside the app for offline reading. When you play audio recitations or translations that are not cached locally, the application makes standard HTTPS requests to public educational Quranic audio repositories to stream the MP3 files. No personal identifiers, advertising IDs, or tracking cookies are attached to these audio requests.

4. No Third-Party Analytics, Trackers, or Advertisements

  • Zero third-party advertisements (No AdMob or ad networks).
  • Zero behavioral tracking SDKs or cross-app tracking.
  • Zero in-app purchases or hidden subscriptions.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Global Compliance)

Burhan Rem is safe and educational for users of all ages, from young children to elders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian contacts us via our support channels, any voluntarily shared correspondence is used solely to resolve the inquiry.

6. Voluntary Support Communications

If you choose to message us via WhatsApp or email for customer support or bug reporting, your contact details and message history are used strictly to troubleshoot your issue and provide assistance.
