Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

At Burhan Rem, we deeply respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled:

  • Local Data Storage: All user progress, star ratings, and memorization settings are stored strictly local to your device using browser and app storage mechanisms. We do not transmit or store your personal progress on external servers.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, track, or share any personal identifying information (PII).
  • Audio Streaming: Audio recitations are streamed securely from public educational repositories to assist in your Quranic recitation practice. No user tracking cookies or analytics are bundled with playback.
